A static page with a live suggestion board

Visitors send ideas for your site. You (or we) mark Yes, Later, or No. Approved ideas land on a public board when the site republishes — same pattern as the live demo on suggestpage.com.

Live demos: suggestpage.com · stevenphilley.com · panatau.com

How it works

  1. Someone submits an idea on your suggestion page.
  2. You get the idea by email and decide Yes / Later / No in chat.
  3. The public board updates from data.json when the site republishes. You keep the files (GitHub Pages–friendly).

Who it’s for

Indie sites, studios, and one-person projects that want a public idea inbox without a CMS. One URL, static files, no login wall for visitors.
