2. The service

2.1 Suggest Page (Page Pack)

Suggest Page is a product that delivers static suggestion pages for your brand or project, including a public board that can display visitor ideas marked Yes, Later, or No.

Typical flow:

  1. 1. A visitor submits an idea on your suggestion page.
  2. 2. The idea is emailed to you (and/or us, as configured for your setup).
  3. 3. You (or we, under Site Care) mark the idea Yes / Later / No.
  4. 4. The public board updates from site data (for example, a data.json file) when the static site is republished.

You keep the static files. The setup is designed to be GitHub Pages–friendly (or similar static hosting). There is no visitor login wall required by the product as generally described.
